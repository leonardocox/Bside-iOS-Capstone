# Bside Capstone Brainstorming

## Initial App Ideas

1. **Bside Music Listening Journal**  
   Search for real songs, albums, and EPs in a music catalog. Rate them, write short reviews, mark first listens, and collect them in a personal listening journal.

2. **Game Backlog Diary**  
   Keep track of games you want to play, are currently playing, or have finished. Add ratings and short thoughts as you make progress.

3. **Song Sketchbook**  
   Capture lyrics, voice recordings, chord ideas, and unfinished song concepts in one place so they are easy to revisit and develop.

4. **Soundtrack of My Life**  
   Connect songs to memories, dates, photos, or places and browse those moments as a personal musical timeline.

5. **Matchday Notebook**  
   Record predictions and reactions to soccer matches, then look back at how your opinions compared with the results.

6. **Game Night Picker**  
   Save a group's game options and help choose something to play based on available time and what everyone is in the mood for.
   
   
## Evaluation of My Top Three Ideas

### Bside Music Listening Journal

**Mobile**  
I could search for a song and record my thoughts soon after hearing it. The app would make it easy to keep my listening journal with me wherever I go.

**Story**  
Music means a lot to me, and I like the idea of remembering what I listened to and how I felt about it. I would enjoy using this app myself and sharing the idea with other music fans.

**Market**  
The app is for people who enjoy discovering music and want a personal place to rate songs and albums and write about them.

**Habit**  
Each new song or album gives someone a reason to come back. They could also revisit older ratings and see how their taste has changed.

**Scope**  
A first version could focus on searching for music, writing a review, giving a rating, and saving the entry. Connecting to a music catalog is the biggest technical challenge, so I would keep other ideas for later.

### Game Backlog Diary

**Mobile**  
Someone could quickly update a game's status or write down their thoughts after a playing session.

**Story**  
Many players have games they mean to finish or want to remember. This app would give them a place to track that journey and reflect on what they played.

**Market**  
The audience would be people who play several games and want to organize their backlog and impressions.

**Habit**  
People could return when they start a game, make progress, finish it, or decide what to play next.

**Scope**  
A useful first version could work with games entered by the user. Searching an outside game catalog would add another technical challenge.

### Song Sketchbook

**Mobile**  
A phone is a useful place to capture a lyric or melody as soon as an idea comes to mind. Voice recording could make that especially useful.

**Story**  
Creative ideas can disappear quickly. This app would help someone save fragments and turn them into complete songs over time.

**Market**  
The audience is smaller but clear. It would be useful for people who write songs or want to start writing them.

**Habit**  
Someone might open it often when inspiration strikes, though that would vary from person to person.

**Scope**  
Saving and organizing written ideas would be manageable. Adding reliable audio recording and connecting many fragments into songs would take more work.


## Final App Idea

I chose Bside because music is something I genuinely care about, and this is an app I would want to use myself. I like the idea of finding a real song or album, recording my first impressions, rating it, and building a personal history of what I have listened to. It has a clear experience I can demonstrate while still leaving room to grow after this class.

The first version will focus on searching for a song or album, recording whether it was a first listen, giving it a rating and short review, and saving it in a personal journal. The biggest challenge will be connecting it to a music catalog, so I want to keep that main experience working before adding anything else.

Eventually, it would be fun to compare reviews with other people and see who has the most niche music taste. That is an idea for the future rather than something I need for the first version.
